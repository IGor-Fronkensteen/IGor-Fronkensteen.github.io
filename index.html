Nürburgring Lap-Time Predictor

Predicting Nordschleife lap times from raw vehicle specs using ML and physics-aware features

Why it matters

Maps power/weight/aero cues to performance in ways that stay intuitive and explainable.

Notes

  • A tuned Random Forest (RF) model reached strong generalization with tight error bands
  • Shapley Additive exPlanations (SHAP) analysis shows specific power & weight as dominant signals
  • Log transforms help linearize key relationships for simpler models
